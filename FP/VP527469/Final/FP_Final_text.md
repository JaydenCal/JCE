## 1.0   Project understanding

Council is building two engineered cells at the Gregadoo Waste Management Centre under a separate construction contract (CT2027004): Cell 3.1, a general (putrescible and non-putrescible) waste cell of about 4 ha, and Monocell M1.1, a cell of about 1.1 ha for special wastes including bonded and friable asbestos, asbestos-contaminated soils and immobilised lead slag. Portion 2 is triggered at the Principal's direction after planning permission. The CQA Engineer verifies that the works are built to the Tonkin Consulting IFC design, the Technical Specifications (201879S0011 and 201879S0032) and the NSW EPA Environmental Guidelines: Solid Waste Landfills (2016), and compiles the as-constructed CQA report that supports EPA acceptance of each cell.

Each cell has a composite liner of engineered fill layer, geosynthetic clay liner (GCL) and HDPE geomembrane, protected by a cushion geotextile, with a groundwater relief system below and a leachate collection system, gravel drainage layer, separation geotextile, drainage geocomposite and woven geotextile above. The CQA Plans (Attachment A, 201879S002 Rev 1, and Attachment E, 201879S004 Rev 2) set the hold points, test frequencies and acceptance criteria the CQA Engineer controls, and require full-time CQA inspection of the geosynthetic lining works.

**Program.** Program is the main constraint. Works within Cell 3.1 must be completed to the satisfaction of the CQA Engineer by 30/09/2027 so the cell can operate from November 2027 (Part D 1.17.5). Hold points must be released quickly, test results turned around within days and the Cell 3.1 report issued promptly.

**Testing.** Under Council's answers of 06/10/2026 and 09/10/2026, the CQA Engineer arranges and prices the independent (IQA) geotechnical and geosynthetic testing at a NATA-accredited laboratory, including sample transport. The Head Contractor supplies samples free of charge, provides the GTA (subgrade) testing and results, and engages the licensed surveyor.

**Site presence.** Part D 1.13 asks for CQA representatives on site during all working hours of the works. The CQA Plans call for full-time CQA inspection during the geosynthetic lining works. We have priced both, as separate provisional sums PS1 and PS2, so Council can see the cost of each and adjust once RFI 1 is answered.

### Construction phase CQA by work element (B2 items 2.1 to 2.14 and 6.1 to 6.14)

- **Earthworks and subgrade.** Technician witnesses proof rolling; JCE reviews GTA results and the Plane 1 survey and releases the subgrade before engineered fill.
- **Groundwater relief system.** Approval of gravel and geotextile, inspection of pipe materials, perforations, trenches, pipe placement and jointing (hold points H-GR2 to H-GR8).
- **Engineered fill layer.** Oversight of the GITA's Level 1 inspection and testing, review of compaction, moisture and permeability results and the Plane 2 survey and isopachytes, release before lining.
- **Anchor trenches.** Inspection of location, depth and width, review of backfill compaction and survey before covering.
- **GCL and HDPE geomembrane.** Full-time CQA inspection throughout deployment. Subgrade acceptance, panel layout and overlaps, trial welds, non-destructive testing of every seam, destructive sample results, repairs, panel and seam records. IQA samples cut and sent to the NATA laboratory at the CQA Plan frequencies.
- **Liner integrity survey.** Coordination and witnessing of the leak location survey on the exposed liner and after the gravel drainage layer, with leaks repaired and retested before release.
- **Geotextiles and geocomposite.** MQA review, IQA sampling, deployment, overlaps, seaming and repairs.
- **Leachate collection and transfer systems.** Pipe materials, jointing and welding, perforation orientation, bedding and pressure test records; gravel drainage layer compliance testing, placement method that protects the liner, and Plane 3 survey.
- **Fencing and supporting infrastructure.** Inspection against the drawings and specification.
