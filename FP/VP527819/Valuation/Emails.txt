Hello,

JCE Infrastructure is tendering for Inner West Council's RFT 47-26, Transport Assets Condition Audit & Revaluations 2026/27 (VendorPanel VP527819), as Prime Contractor. I am looking for a valuation partner for the comprehensive revaluation.

Council requires a revaluation of roads, footpaths, kerb and gutter, traffic devices, street furniture, artwork, car parks and white way lighting under AASB 13, AASB 116, AASB 136 and AASB 2022-10, following the IPWEA guidance, with working papers and draft and final reports by 31/03/2027. JCE will supply the audit data and engineering inputs. The attached RFQ (JCE_RFQ_Valuation_IWC-Transport-Audit_Final_APV.pdf) asks you to price two options: (1) review and sign-off of JCE's revaluation by your qualified valuer, or (2) a full revaluation by your team, with artwork valuation advice and audit support after March 2027.

The RFQ also asks for hourly rates, exclusions, insurances at Council's levels (public liability $20 million, professional indemnity $10 million), 90-day price validity, the name and CV of the signing valuer, two to three comparable NSW council revaluations with referees, and a declaration of any previous valuation work for Inner West Council or any bid of your own.

Key dates:

- Questions to JCE: 12:00 pm Friday 16/10/2026
- Quote due to JCE: 12:00 pm Wednesday 21/10/2026
- Tender closes: 2:00 pm Wednesday 28/10/2026 (no further extensions)

Engagement depends on JCE winning the tender and Council approving your firm as a subcontractor. I am happy to talk it through by phone.
