Hi Mark,

JCE Infrastructure is preparing a quotation for Mid-Western Regional Council's RFQ2026_93 (eProcure MWRCCMS001497): a review of the 2019 Contributions Plan and a new Section 7.11 and Section 7.12 Contributions Plan, with car parking, REZ and OSOM / heavy haulage provisions and a Staff Contributions Administration Guide. Delivery is November 2026 to May 2027. JCE will be lead consultant and will cover the infrastructure works schedules, engineering costs, car parking cost basis and heavy haulage contribution.

I am looking for a planning partner to lead the planning content. Council's RFQ makes NSW contributions plan experience a minimum requirement, so please include the named staff who would do the work, two to three NSW Section 7.11 / 7.12 plans they prepared or reviewed with a council referee for each, and your consent to be named in our submission (section 2 of the attached RFQ).

The scope, insurance (PL $20M; subconsultant PI default $10M under Council's contract, which we have asked Council to review) and pricing by Council's three stages are in the attached RFQ Rev B, which replaces any earlier version. The quote return form is attached as well.

Council's RFQ closes at 2:00pm Monday 19/10/2026. Could you send your quote by 12:00pm Thursday 15/10/2026? If that is too tight, an indicative fee, your named lead planner with their NSW contributions plan experience, and a conflict declaration by then would let me hold a place for you.

As you act on applications in the Mid-Western LGA, please complete the independence declaration in section 8 of the RFQ.

Any engagement depends on JCE winning the work and Council approving subconsultants.

Please call me on 0477 207 027 if you would like to talk it through.
