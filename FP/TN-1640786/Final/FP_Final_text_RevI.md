## 1. Understanding and expected benefits to Council

Council's Contributions Plan was adopted in 2019. Since then growth rates and patterns, strategic priorities and infrastructure needs across the region have changed. Council's adopted Housing Strategy and Employment Lands Strategy, the future Mudgee Town Centre Growth Strategy, and the Central-West Orana Renewable Energy Zone (REZ) all bear on where development will occur and what infrastructure it will need. The 2019 Plan also has gaps: there is no contribution for car parking in the Mudgee CBD, and Council's draft Heavy Vehicle (OSOM) Contributions Plan sits outside it.

Council wants a new Section 7.11 and Section 7.12 Contributions Plan that reflects current and likely future development, aligns with its strategic plans and infrastructure priorities, and is clear and practical for staff to administer.

### How JCE will deliver the expected benefits

| Benefit sought by Council | How JCE will achieve it |
|---|---|
| An updated framework that reflects current development, infrastructure needs and strategic priorities | Growth and land release taken from Council's adopted strategies; every works item checked against Council's Delivery Program, Operational Plan and infrastructure planning documents; works costed on one documented basis and reviewed by Sunny Lotay before issue. |
| Clearer and more consistent application of contributions | Plain-English plan structure; clear tables of contribution rates by development type and catchment; exemptions, credits and timing of payment set out in one place; a Staff Contributions Administration Guide with worked examples. |
| A plan Council can administer and defend | Nexus and apportionment documented for each item; calculations in an editable spreadsheet Council keeps; IPART threshold checked before exhibition; GIS layers for MapInfo. |
| Gaps in the 2019 Plan addressed | Car parking contribution rate built from the cost per space in the Mudgee Town Centre; the draft OSOM plan reviewed and incorporated; REZ-related impacts tested in Stage 2. |

### Key risks and how JCE will manage them

| Risk | JCE response |
|---|---|
| Christmas and New Year shutdown delays Stage 2 | Stage 2 data requests issued in Stage 1; discussion paper drafted before the shutdown and finalised in January 2027. |
| Council meeting cycle and exhibition push out the final plan | Council's meeting dates confirmed at inception and the program built backwards from the target adoption meeting in May 2027. |
| Works costs out of date or inconsistent | One costing basis (unit rates, on-costs, design and contingency allowances), documented and independently reviewed. |
| Heavy haulage and REZ contributions are hard to defend | Rates built from haulage volumes, routes, ESA loading and pavement costs, building on Council's draft OSOM plan; options tested in the Stage 2 discussion paper before any rate is set. |
| Rates exceed the IPART review threshold | Rates checked against the threshold at the draft stage so Council can decide on scope or apportionment before exhibition. |

## 3. Innovations and alternative approaches

JCE will test the following in the Stage 2 discussion paper. Each is an option for Council to accept or reject, and none changes the price.

- Heavy haulage: a rate per tonne-kilometre on defined haul routes, so the contribution follows the road damage each development causes, building on Council's draft OSOM plan.
- REZ and other large temporary workforces: options to capture construction traffic and short-term infrastructure demand through the Section 7.12 levy, conditions or planning agreements, for Council's decision.
- Car parking: a cash-in-lieu rate per space based on construction and land cost in the Mudgee Town Centre, with the works linked to the Town Centre Growth Strategy.
- Administration: an indexed calculation spreadsheet with the Administration Guide, so staff can calculate a contribution and check credits in one step.
