## Bid / no-bid view on the auditor requirement

Council is buying a statutory environmental auditor, not an engineer: the appointee is the auditor of record with EPA, and 40% of the score (EPA audit experience 20%, key personnel with current auditor appointment 20%) plus much of the 15% independence criterion rests on the auditor alone. A JCE-led bid stacks JCE's fee on top of the auditor's in a 30% price criterion, needs Council's clause 20.3 approval for the auditor as subconsultant, scores nothing for local purchasing, and leaves JCE carrying the Part B indemnity for an audit it cannot perform. An auditor bidding direct will almost certainly beat it. My view: send the auditor RFQs today because they are worded to allow either structure, but treat a JCE-led bid as unlikely. If an auditor prefers to tender direct and wants civil and hydraulic support, take the smaller subconsultant role (option B). If no auditor commits by 16/10/2026, or Council answers RFI 1 that the tenderer must be the auditor, do not bid, and hold the geotechnical RFQs back unless an auditor agrees to a JCE-led structure.
