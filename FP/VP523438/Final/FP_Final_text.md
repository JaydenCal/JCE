## 1.0   Project understanding

Hawthorn Road runs from the Adelaide Road intersection in Mount Barker past Canberra Street, the Southern Cross Care retirement village, Silverwood Drive and Bollen Road, then past the 147 Hawthorn Road link road and a future roundabout to Liebelt Summer Track: about 3 km of road in the eight sections shown in Brief Figures 1 to 8. The road has narrow lanes, failing pavement, steep and uneven verges, no street lighting, stobie poles close to the carriageway and little provision for people walking or cycling. Development at #16, #73, #78, #147, #160 and #239 Hawthorn Road, and DIT's upgrade of the Adelaide Road intersection, will make it a key collector or sub-arterial road.

Council's objective for Separable Portion 1 is a 30% concept that defines and documents the preferred upgrade. It has to balance road safety, traffic operation, active transport, stormwater and flooding, services, trees and remnant vegetation, land requirements, constructability and whole-of-life cost, and give Council a cost estimate, land acquisition plan and staging plan it can use for budgeting, land negotiations and detailed design.

The methodology follows Brief sections 3 to 6 in three stages that match Council's milestones (Brief Table 1) and the fee breakdown in Section 8.0. Work marked [Sub] is delivered by a JCE sub-consultant within the lump sum.

### Stage 1: Start-up, investigations and Concept Design Basis (to the 10% workshop, 12/02/2027)

- Start-up meeting with Council and a two-day site appraisal of the whole corridor: drainage lines, culvert condition, verges, accesses, stobie poles, trees and interfaces, with a photo log (by 04/12/2026). JCE will also fly the corridor with its RTK drone and process the capture in Autodesk ReCap Pro into an orthophoto and a terrain surface of existing conditions (verges, accesses, culvert inlets and outlets, trees and stobie poles); the ReCap surface feeds the InfraWorks and Civil 3D concept design model as context for the options and workshops, and supplements the engineering survey without replacing it. High-resolution aerial stills and video from the same flights form part of the existing-condition record and are used in Council's workshop presentations.
- Project management plan, program, risk register and Safety in Design hazard register.
- Collection and review of DIT's intersection design, Council's CWMS and stormwater layout plans, the approved #73 CHR design, the Bollen Road closure, the link road and roundabout options, development plans, AADT and crash data.
- BYDA search and services conflict register; liaison with SA Power Networks, SA Water, NBN Co and the gas network operator.
- Engineering survey on GDA2020 MGA Zone 54 and AHD, with underground services location to Quality Level B, a 3D tree survey model and property boundaries, delivered as A1 plans, DWG, DTM and breaklines (Brief 3.2 and 3.3) [Sub].
- Geotechnical investigation scope agreed with Council before work on site, then boreholes, testing and a pavement design report using Council's AADT (Brief 3.4) [Sub].
- Concept Design Basis Report: design and posted speeds, design and check vehicles agreed with Council, cross-section elements for each section, standards and departures, drainage and flood criteria, lighting categories, and the interfaces with others.
- 10% design briefing workshop with Council's stakeholders.

### Stage 2: Options and preliminary 30% concept design (to the workshop on 09/04/2027)

- Optioneering of feasible 2D concepts and a multi-criteria assessment (Brief 3.1): roundabout location east of the creek with access to #16; Silverwood Drive CHR against closure with a cul-de-sac or hammerhead; CHR warrants at the retirement village and #78; the north-south trail crossing; interim and ultimate layouts.
- Civil 3D corridor model, horizontal and vertical concept, typical cross-sections for each section, and the feasibility of the verge earthworks about 40 m to 210 m west of Silverwood Drive (Brief 3.5).
- Intersection, CHR and CHL layouts with tie-ins to DIT's intersection, the #73 CHR, the Bollen Road closure and the link road and roundabout; swept paths and sight distance at intersections, accesses and crossings.
- Shared path and footpath layout, DDA compliance as far as practicable, and stobie pole clearances.
- Stormwater (Brief 3.6): full contributing catchments, existing provisions, minor (10% AEP) and major (1% AEP) systems, overland flow paths, a preliminary flood assessment at the creek crossings, 1% AEP capacity and condition of the existing culverts with upgrade requirements, SSP2-4.5 (2100) with SSP3-7.0 sensitivity, and WSUD treatment of road runoff.
- Traffic assessment with SIDRA modelling of the key intersections, a Safe System Assessment of the options, and a preliminary 30% road safety audit by an independent auditor [Sub].
- Tree Assessment Report prepared with Council's arborist, and refinement of the alignment to retain high value trees (Brief 3.9) [Sub].
- Street lighting concept to AS/NZS 1158: category V5 from Canberra Street to the link road and PR5 from the link road to Liebelt Summer Track, with luminaire and pole locations and electrical requirements (Brief 3.8) [Sub].
- Preliminary 30% drawings, preliminary Concept Design Report and cost estimate, and a design workshop with Council's stakeholders.

### Stage 3: Final concept design (to completion, 18/06/2027)

- Responses to Council's consolidated comments and to the road safety audit and Safe System Assessment, recorded in the comments register.
- Traffic control devices, signage and linemarking concept and the traffic control plan (Brief 3.7).
- Safety in Design workshop and report (Brief 3.11).
- Land acquisition plan (Brief 3.10) and project staging plan for the interim and ultimate layouts.
- Final concept drawings (PDF and AutoCAD), final Concept Design Report, final road safety audit, and a concept cost estimate covering pavement, paths, drainage, traffic control, service alterations, lighting, landscaping and ancillary works (Brief 3.12), with a concept streetscape and landscape plan to Council's scope.
- Monthly online progress meetings throughout.
