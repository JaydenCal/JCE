## Drone photogrammetry
H2: Show the site. Sell the vision.
Bring the existing site and your proposal into one clear presentation.
Drone photographs become a textured 3D model of the terrain and surrounding buildings. Add sheds, new buildings, proposed lots and stages to show buyers and funders how the project could take shape.
Animation: "Cross-grid flight · first pass · Demo site · process illustration", photo counter, Play the process, 0:00 / 0:30, Replay
01 Capture — Fly a criss-cross grid. Take overlapping photos from different viewpoints.
02 Align — Match shared features across the photographs and locate the camera positions.
03 Reconstruct — Build the point cloud, surface mesh and textured model of the existing site.
04 Model the proposal — Position the shed, build the frame, then add walls, roof and openings from the drawings.
05 Present — Show the proposed building in context for early marketing and preliminary funding discussions.
Early marketing — Introduce proposed buildings, lots and the site setting before construction begins.
Preliminary funding — Give funders and partners a clear view of the proposal alongside your project documents.
Project communication — Explain the layout, access, terrain and proposed stages to buyers and stakeholders.
Note: Illustrative site capture and proposal modelling, shown in time-lapse. The shed is a concept example. Capture, drawings and presentation outputs are tailored to each project.
