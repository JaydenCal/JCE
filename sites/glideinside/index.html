How it works

Three steps, and you don't need to leave the office.

Questions

What plan formats do you accept?

PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 20 MB. A plan with room names and dimensions gives the best result. If anything is hard to read, we email you before we start.

How long does it take?

Your video arrives within 48 hours of us receiving your plan and payment.

Do you need to visit the property?

No. We build everything from the plan, which is why it works for homes that haven't been built yet.

How does payment work?

You pay $249 by card through a secure Stripe checkout when you upload your plan. There are no invoices to chase. Volume orders are quoted first, and you pay once you've accepted the quote.

Who owns the video?

You can use the video to market that property on any channel. [TBC: confirm ownership and licence terms]

Can I track my order?

Yes. You get an order reference on screen when you submit the form, and it's in your confirmation email. Reply to that email with your reference at any time and we'll tell you where your video is up to.

What if I need changes?

One round of changes is included [TBC], such as furniture style, finishes or room names. Changes to the floor plan itself after modelling has started are quoted separately.

Do you work in my area?

We work with agents and builders in regional New South Wales, with other states to follow. We don't take work in capital cities.
