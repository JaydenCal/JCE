Drone photogrammetry

Show the site. Sell the vision.

Bring the existing site and your proposal into one clear presentation. Drone photographs become a textured 3D model of the terrain and surrounding buildings. Add sheds, new buildings, proposed lots and stages to show buyers and funders how the project could take shape.

Drone capture animationPlaceholder: the live 30-second process animation goes here [TBC]

  1. Capture

    Fly a criss-cross grid and take overlapping photos from different viewpoints.

  2. Align

    Match shared features across the photographs and locate the camera positions.

  3. Reconstruct

    Build the point cloud, surface mesh and textured model of the existing site.

  4. Model the proposal

    Position the shed, build the frame, then add walls, roof and openings from the drawings.

  5. Present

    Show the proposed building in context for early marketing and preliminary funding discussions.

What floor plan do you need?

A PDF, JPG or PNG of the plan you already have. Working drawings and sales plans both work. We contact you only if the plan needs checking.

Does the $249 fly-through include the site, stormwater or levels?

No. The $249 price covers the inside of the home only. Stormwater, levels, retaining walls, driveways, landscaping and facades are modelled as a site model and quoted separately.

How long does it take?

A single home interior fly-through is delivered within 5 business days. For other projects, we reply with a quote within one business day.

Do you offer volume pricing for builders?

Yes. Send the whole range at once and we quote per design within one business day, keeping the look consistent across every video.

Can you show the facade and the home on its lot?

Yes, as a site model. The facade, the lot and outside works aren't part of the $249 interior price, so we quote them separately.
