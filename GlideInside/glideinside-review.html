Common questions

Is this approved?

No. Pocket Architect produces concept designs only. They have not been checked against planning controls, the National Construction Code, BASIX or any standard.

Can I lodge the plans with council?

No. A qualified designer or certifier must prepare development application or complying development documentation. You can use your concept plan as a starting brief for them.

Do I need an account?

No account is planned for launch. Projects autosave in your browser, and you can save the project file to your computer.

Can I print the PDF plans more than once?

Each job includes one set of PDF plans. The set is generated once, on our server, when you export it, and is locked to that job. Keep the zip file safe: it holds your plans and files, and you can load it back into the site to carry on with the job.

What is a credit?

A credit measures how much of the AI assistant a request uses. A Plan Pack includes 500 AI credits for one project. Credits have no cash value and expire with the Plan Pack. When they run out you can keep editing and exporting by hand.

What is the refund policy?

Placeholder wording, to be confirmed in the order terms: refund on request within 14 days if no paid export has been downloaded. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law are not affected.

Before we begin

Who provides the feasibility support?

Glide Inside’s director is a licensed civil engineer and can assist with preliminary subdivision feasibility. The work and the outputs are confirmed around your site and brief.

What should I send first?

The property location, your proposed use or lot concept, and any survey, site plans or existing models you have. Add the questions you want to explore and your project timing.

What stage is this work for?

Preliminary feasibility, concept development and project communication. We discuss how you intend to use the work and what further information or specialist input the project needs as it develops.

Can I lodge this with council?

No. The concept is not a survey plan, a development application or engineering design. It helps you organise the early questions and explain the idea. A registered surveyor and your council remain the source for boundaries, levels and planning requirements.

Concepts are for discussion only. They are not survey or development application documentation and do not replace engineering design, planning advice or legal advice.
