What you get

The plan pack outputs below are generated on the server from your project. The Plan Pack is not on sale yet.

PDF

A3 plan set

A3 landscape vector sheets at 1:100 (or 1:50 or 1:200 to fit), with dimension strings, north arrow, scale bar, door and window schedule, area schedule and a cover sheet with a 3D snapshot.

DXF

CAD file

ASCII DXF (R12) in millimetres, with plan items on separate named layers. Opens in AutoCAD, BricsCAD, DraftSight and LibreCAD.

3D

3D model

GLB as the main format, plus OBJ with MTL in a zip. Opens in Windows 3D Viewer, Blender and most 3D tools.

How it works

  1. Draw

    Draw rooms and walls on a snapping grid, then place doors, windows, stairs, decks and tanks.

  2. See in 3D and walk through

    Switch to 3D at any time, take the roof off, then walk through the model in first person.

  3. Furnish and finish

    Add furniture and pick materials and colours for walls, floors and roofs.

  4. Export

    With a Plan Pack, export a PDF plan set, a DXF and a 3D model.

Common questions

Is this approved?

No. Plan Studio produces concept designs only. They have not been checked against planning controls, the National Construction Code, BASIX or any standard.

Can I lodge the plans with council?

No. A qualified designer or certifier must prepare development application or complying development documentation. You can use your concept plan as a starting brief for them.

Do I need an account?

No account is planned for launch. Projects autosave in your browser, and you can save the project file to your computer.

What is a credit?

A credit measures how much of the AI assistant a request uses. A Plan Pack includes 500 AI credits for one project. Credits have no cash value and expire with the Plan Pack. When they run out you can keep editing and exporting by hand.

What is the refund policy?
