# Review B: residential app flow

Reviewed 18/11/2026 on branch claude/site-review-bc (from release-6). Method: Playwright driving the guided flow on the sample lot (stubbed /api/site, tools/resiflow-harness.cjs) at 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844, then the editor, Exports menu and help. An in-page audit (tools/review-audit.cjs) checked contrast (WCAG AA), controls without names, clipped text, dashes, the word "compliant" and prices on every screen. Screenshots are in /home/claude/shots/site-review-bc/ (res/ is the first pass, after/ is after the fixes).

Flow result: find lot, choose home, place (nudge, turn, mirror, best fit, drag), check, open editor, Back to placement and a refresh at step 3 all worked at 1440 (tools/resiflow-shots.cjs checks passed up to the commercial stage). No dead buttons found. No unlabeled controls found. No dashes and no "compliant" in any screen. Prices seen: Plan Pack A$30 per job (Exports menu), AI top-up A$15 for 100 messages (src/premium/credits.js), both correct.

Severity: High blocks or badly misleads, Medium confuses or hurts a task, Low polish. Status: Fixed here, or Recommend (design change, not done).
