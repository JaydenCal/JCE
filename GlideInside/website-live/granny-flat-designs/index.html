Granny flats and the NSW rules

In NSW a granny flat is a secondary dwelling. Under the Housing SEPP 2021 and the Codes SEPP, a secondary dwelling is generally limited to 60 m² of floor area outside rural zones. The minimum lot size for complying development is 450 m².

Approval is always required. If all the standards are met it can be a complying development certificate from council or a private certifier. Otherwise a development application is needed. Always confirm the details with your council.

Common questions

What is a granny flat?

A granny flat is a small, self-contained home on the same lot as a main house. In NSW the planning term is a secondary dwelling.

How big can a granny flat be in NSW?

Under the Housing SEPP 2021 and the Codes SEPP, a secondary dwelling is generally limited to 60 m² of floor area outside rural zones. Always confirm the limits for your lot with your council.

Do I need approval to build a granny flat?

Yes, approval is always required in NSW. It can be a complying development certificate from council or a private certifier if all the standards are met, or a development application. The minimum lot size for complying development is 450 m². Confirm with your council.

Can I design a 1 bedroom granny flat?

Yes. Open the 2 bedroom template and remove a bedroom, or start from a blank plan and draw only one. You can also upload a JPG or PNG plan and trace it.
