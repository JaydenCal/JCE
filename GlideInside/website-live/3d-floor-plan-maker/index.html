About this floor plan maker

Pocket Architect is a floor plan maker and house plan maker for the browser. Draw a floor plan online by adding rooms and walls, then place doors, windows, stairs, decks and rainwater tanks.

The 3D model builds as you draw. Orbit it, take the roof off, change finishes and walk through in first person. It is a quick way to test a layout before you brief a designer.

Start from a template or trace a plan

As a floor plan designer it offers starting templates: granny flat, shed, single storey house, duplex and extension starter, or a blank plan.

If you already have a plan, upload a JPG or PNG and trace over it to rebuild it in 3D.

Free to draw, pay when you export

Drawing and viewing are free, with no sign-up, and your work saves in the browser. A$35 per job (incl. GST) unlocks the export, and you only pay at the end.

The export is a PDF plan set, a DXF for CAD, a 3D model file and an interactive HTML file that opens offline, plus 500 AI assistant credits. The Glide Inside team also offers a 3D fly-through video for A$10 and complex renderings for A$10.

Common questions

Is the floor plan maker free?

Drawing and viewing in 3D are free, with no sign-up. You only pay at the end, when you export. A$35 per job (incl. GST) unlocks the export.

What can I export?

For A$35 per job (incl. GST) you get a PDF plan set (A3 sheets to scale, with schedules and a title block), a DXF for CAD, a 3D model file (GLB and OBJ) and an interactive HTML file that opens offline, plus 500 AI assistant credits. You can export again as often as you like for 12 months.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

It runs in the browser and works best on a computer.

Can I upload my own plan?

Yes. Upload a JPG or PNG of your plan and trace over it to rebuild it in the tool.

Are the plans approved?

No. They are concept designs only. They are not construction drawings, not development application or complying development documents, and have not been checked against planning controls, the NCC or BASIX.
