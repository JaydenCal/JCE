Privacy policy

Last updated 19/11/2026

1. Who we are

Glide Inside, ABN 11 549 431 925, provides the Glide Inside software, sold as Glide Inside Residential and Glide Inside Commercial. Contact: hello@glideinside.com.au. In this policy, we, us and our mean Glide Inside, ABN 11 549 431 925.

2. What we collect

  • Glide Inside plans: autosave stays in your browser on your device. Plan data is sent to our server only when you use an export (PDF, DXF, 3D model), the AI assistant or Save and share.
  • Save and share: the design is stored on our server so the link works on any device, for up to 12 months after it was last saved. Anyone with the link can view it. If you add an email address there, we store it with the design and do not send emails to it.
  • Free profiles: if you create one, the details in section 3 below.
  • Purchases: your email address, used only to restore your purchase and send receipts. Stripe collects payment details. We do not see or store card numbers.
  • Enquiries: details you send by email or an order form, such as your name, contact details, project notes and uploaded plans.
  • Website measurement: when analytics are switched on, Google Analytics and Google Ads record how visitors use this site and whether a visit leads to a design, a purchase or an enquiry. They use cookies and similar identifiers. We do not send your plans or card details to them.

3. Free profiles

A free profile is optional. You can use Glide Inside without one. If you create one, we store:

  • Your name, email address and password. The password is kept only as a one way hash. We cannot read it and we never email it.
  • Optional details you choose to give: a phone number, a postcode or suburb, and whether you are a homeowner, builder, developer or other.
  • The designs you save to your profile: the design itself, its name, the site address if you looked one up, and a small picture of the plan. If you bought a Plan Pack while signed in, we link it to your profile so it works on another device.
  • The date you accepted the site terms and this privacy policy.
  • Your marketing choice, with the date and the exact wording you agreed to.

Marketing is opt in. The box that asks if you would like occasional Glide Inside updates and offers is never ticked for you. We only send marketing to people who ticked it, and you can change your mind at any time under Email preferences in your profile or by contacting us. Creating a profile does not send you any email. If you do not tick the box, we use your email only to run your profile and to reply to you.

Deleting your account. Choose Delete my account in your profile. This deletes your profile, your saved designs, the links to any Plan Pack, your sign in sessions and your marketing record straight away. You can also ask us by email. Our database provider may keep backups for a short time, up to 30 days, after that. Designs kept only in your browser, and share links you made, are separate: a share link stays open until it expires, as described above.

4. How we use it

  • To deliver exports and AI results, restore purchases, issue receipts and reply to enquiries.
  • Plans and drawings you send us are used only for your project. We do not publish them or use them as examples without your written permission.
  • We do not sell personal information.

5. Who handles data for us

  • Stripe: payments.
  • Cloudflare: hosting of the Glide Inside service, storage and delivery of exports.
  • Anthropic: AI assistant requests are processed in the United States. Plan data needed for the request is sent to it.
  • VentraIP, an Australian provider: this website and our business mailbox.
  • Google: website analytics and advertising measurement.
  • JCE Infrastructure Pty Ltd: receives engineering order requests (section 7).
  • The builder whose widget you used, and any service the builder has set up to receive their leads (section 6).

Some of these providers store or process data outside Australia.

6. Builder website widgets and leads

Some builders show a Glide Inside widget ("See it on your block") on their own website. A visitor enters an address, tries the builder's designs on the block and can choose to send their details to that builder.

  • What we keep: the name, email address and optional phone number you type, the block address and lot you looked up, the design you chose, the website that showed the widget, and a record of your consent (the wording, its version and the time you ticked the box).
  • Who receives it: the builder whose widget it is. We store the lead for that builder on their behalf, in their Glide Inside portal. The builder decides how to use it, so their own privacy policy applies to what they do with it.
  • Webhooks and Zapier: a builder can ask us to forward each new lead to a web address or service they choose, such as Zapier, a CRM or a spreadsheet. We send it signed, over https, to the address the builder set up. We do not control what that service does with it.
  • We do not send you any email, and we do not use these details for our own marketing. Nothing is sent unless you tick the consent box and press send.
  • The widget does not run Google Analytics and does not report to the website around it.

7. Engineering order requests

The Order engineering form in Glide Inside lets you ask for engineering work such as a stormwater concept, a site classification or a footing design. The request is not a quote or a contract. When you send one, your name, email address, optional phone number, the services you chose, the job (address, lot, design name and a link to the design, if you have one), your notes, and your consent record are sent to JCE Infrastructure Pty Ltd, ABN 29 699 165 663, a separate business that provides the engineering. JCE Infrastructure uses them only to contact you about your request. Glide Inside does not send you any email about it.

8. Keeping data

We keep records as long as needed to deliver work and meet tax and business obligations. Some records have a set limit:

  • Widget leads and engineering order requests are deleted 24 months after they were made.
  • The log of webhook deliveries (when each lead was sent, whether it arrived, and any error, with no lead details) is deleted after 90 days.
  • A builder can delete a lead from their inbox at any time, and a lead is deleted with the builder's account.

9. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information by emailing hello@glideinside.com.au. To have a widget lead deleted, tell us the email address you used and the builder's name, and we will delete it from the builder's inbox and the delivery log. To have an engineering order request deleted, give us the reference shown when you sent it, or the email address you used. A copy a builder has already passed to their own CRM or spreadsheet is theirs to delete, so you may also need to ask the builder. If we cannot resolve a complaint, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).
