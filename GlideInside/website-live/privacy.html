Privacy policy

Last updated 14/10/2026

1. Who we are

Glide Inside, ABN 11 549 431 925, provides Pocket Architect. Contact: [email protected].

2. What we collect

  • Pocket Architect plans: autosave stays in your browser on your device. Plan data is sent to our server only when you use an export (PDF, DXF, 3D model), the AI assistant or Save and share.
  • Save and share: the design is stored on our server so the link works on any device, for up to 12 months after it was last saved. Anyone with the link can view it. If you add an email address there, we store it with the design and do not send emails to it.
  • Purchases: your email address, used only to restore your purchase and send receipts. Stripe collects payment details. We do not see or store card numbers.
  • Enquiries: details you send by email or an order form, such as your name, contact details, project notes and uploaded plans.
  • Website measurement: when analytics are switched on, Google Analytics and Google Ads record how visitors use this site and whether a visit leads to a design, a purchase or an enquiry. They use cookies and similar identifiers. We do not send your plans or card details to them.

3. How we use it

  • To deliver exports and AI results, restore purchases, issue receipts and reply to enquiries.
  • Plans and drawings you send us are used only for your project. We do not publish them or use them as examples without your written permission.
  • We do not sell personal information.

4. Who handles data for us

  • Stripe: payments.
  • Cloudflare: hosting of the Pocket Architect service, storage and delivery of exports.
  • Anthropic: AI assistant requests are processed in the United States. Plan data needed for the request is sent to it.
  • VentraIP, an Australian provider: this website and our business mailbox.
  • Google: website analytics and advertising measurement.

Some of these providers store or process data outside Australia.

5. Keeping data

We keep records as long as needed to deliver work and meet tax and business obligations.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information by emailing [email protected]. If we cannot resolve a complaint, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).
