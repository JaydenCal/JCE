About Glide Inside

Site planning and house design software for regional New South Wales and Queensland.

Glide Inside is site planning and house design software for regional New South Wales and Queensland. It is built by a small team in regional NSW, led by a qualified civil engineer. We made it because the first question on most builds, how will it sit on my block, usually costs money and weeks to answer. Glide Inside answers it as a concept, in a browser, before you commit to design work.

What it is, and what it is not

Glide Inside produces concept layouts and concept plan sets. They are a way to settle the layout and the siting first. They are not a survey, a development application, a building approval, or engineering, legal or financial advice, and they are not checked against planning controls or the National Construction Code.

Where the data comes from

Lot boundaries, aerial imagery and elevation data come from NSW Spatial Services and the State of Queensland. Setback rules are paraphrased from public council documents and are indicative. Every source, date and limit is listed on how accurate it is.
