Pricing questions

Is it really free to draw?

Yes. The 2D editor, 3D view and walk through are free with no sign-up. You pay when you want the PDF plans, DXF and 3D model.

What does a Plan Pack include?

A PDF plan set, a DXF for CAD, a 3D model (GLB and OBJ), an interactive HTML file that opens offline and 500 AI credits for one job. You can export again as often as you like for 12 months. Each job is A$30 including GST.

How do builders pay?

Free 14 day trial for builder staff. Then client logins are A$30 incl. GST each, A$25 each after 15 in a month, talk to us above 50, charged when your client first signs in, billed monthly, with no monthly fee. During the trial there are no client logins or AI credit top-ups; when it ends the account is read only until you add card details. Each login includes a monthly AI allowance that stops when it is used up. AI credit top-ups are A$10 per 300 credits, added to your monthly invoice. Volume pricing is available on request.

Do prices include GST?

Yes. Every price on this site includes GST.

How much is a subdivision concept?

Subdivision work is priced by quote because it depends on the site.
