Pricing questions

Is it really free to draw?

Yes. The 2D editor, 3D view and walk through are free with no sign up. You pay when you want the PDF plans, DXF and 3D model.

What does a Plan Pack include?

A PDF plan set, a DXF for CAD, a 3D model (GLB and OBJ), an interactive HTML file that opens offline, a siting report PDF and 500 AI credits for one job. You can export again as often as you like for 12 months. Each job is A$30 including GST. Plan sets are concept only and are issued under the Plan set terms of use. See a sample plan set

What does Commercial Developer include?

Solo is A$50 per month including GST. Pro (A$149) and Team (A$349) are coming soon. Unlimited commercial concept plan sets (sheets C000 to C104) and DXF drawings for your own projects, plus 150 AI assistant messages each month. Extra messages are A$15 including GST for 100. The subscription renews automatically each month until you cancel. Cancel anytime from Manage billing. Yield, value and cost figures are estimates only.

How do builders pay?

Free 14 day trial for builder staff. Then pay A$20 incl. GST per job, with no monthly fee. A job is one new project: you see the price and confirm before it is created, and it is charged to your saved card. Opening and saving the same project again is free, and client logins are free. Capped monthly plans (Starter A$119 with 10 jobs, Pro A$219 with 20, Teams A$419 with 40) are coming soon, so a busy builder is never worse off than the monthly cap. Extra jobs over a plan are A$20 each and unused jobs do not roll over. During the trial jobs are free and there are no AI credit top ups; when it ends the account is read only until you add card details. Each login includes a monthly AI allowance that stops when it is used up. AI top ups are 100 messages for A$15 incl. GST, added to your monthly invoice.

Do prices include GST?

Yes. Every price on this site includes GST.

How do add-ons work?

The 3D fly-through and complex renderings are A$10 each. You order them by email and we reply with a secure payment link. Length, number of views and turnaround are confirmed before you pay.

How much is a subdivision concept?

Subdivision work is priced by quote because it depends on the site.
