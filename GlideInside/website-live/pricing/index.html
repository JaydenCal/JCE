Pricing questions

Is it really free to draw?

Yes. The 2D editor, 3D view and walk through are free with no sign up. You pay when you want the PDF plans, DXF and 3D model.

What does a Plan Pack include?

A PDF plan set, a DXF for CAD, a 3D model (GLB and OBJ), an interactive HTML file that opens offline and 500 AI credits for one job. You can export again as often as you like for 12 months. Each job is A$30 including GST. Plan sets are concept only and are issued under the Plan set terms of use.

What does Commercial Developer include?

Solo is A$50 per month including GST. Pro (A$149) and Team (A$349) are coming soon. Unlimited commercial concept plan sets (sheets C000 to C104) and DXF drawings for your own projects, plus 150 AI assistant messages each month. Extra messages are A$15 including GST for 100. The subscription renews automatically each month until you cancel. Cancel anytime from Manage billing. Yield, value and cost figures are estimates only.

How do builders pay?

Free 14 day trial for builder staff. Then pay per client login: A$30 incl. GST each for the first 15 in a month, A$25 each from the 16th to the 50th, and custom pricing above 50. Capped monthly plans (Starter A$149 with 10 client logins, Pro A$249 with 20, Teams A$449 with 40) are coming soon, so a busy builder is never worse off than the monthly cap. A login is charged when your client first signs in, billed monthly, with no monthly fee. During the trial there are no client logins or AI credit top ups; when it ends the account is read only until you add card details. Each login includes a monthly AI allowance that stops when it is used up. AI credit top ups are A$10 per 300 credits, added to your monthly invoice.

Do prices include GST?

Yes. Every price on this site includes GST.

How do add-ons work?

The 3D fly-through and complex renderings are A$10 each. You order them by email and we reply with a secure payment link. Length, number of views and turnaround are confirmed before you pay.

How much is a subdivision concept?

Subdivision work is priced by quote because it depends on the site.
