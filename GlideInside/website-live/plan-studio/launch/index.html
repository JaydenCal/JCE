What Pocket Architect does.

Draw your plan on a grid and the 3D model builds as you go. Furnish it, walk through it, then unlock the deliverables below.

Modelling

Plan to 3D

Draw rooms, walls, doors, windows, stairs, decks and tanks. Orbit the 3D model, take the roof off, change finishes and walk through in first person.
