Before you start

What Pocket Architect does.

Draw your plan on a grid and the 3D model builds as you go. Furnish it, walk through it, then unlock the deliverables below.

01 · MODELLING

Plan to 3D

Draw rooms, walls, doors, windows, stairs, decks and tanks. Orbit the 3D model, take the roof off, change finishes and walk through in first person.

02 · PLANS

PDF and DXF

Dimensioned plan sheets with schedules and a title block, plus a DXF for your CAD software. One PDF set per job.

03 · FLY-THROUGH

Walk-through video

A furnished video tour of your design, made by us. Add it to any plan for A$10.

04 · RENDERS

Complex renderings

Detailed still renderings of the finished design, made by us. Add them to any plan for A$10.

Drawing and viewing are free with no sign-up. Unlocking a job is A$35 per job. Concept design only: not for construction and not a development application drawing.
