    What you get

    The Plan Pack outputs below are generated on the server from your project.

    PDF

    A3 plan set

    A3 landscape vector sheets at 1:100 (or 1:50 or 1:200 to fit), with dimension strings, north arrow, scale bar, a room area schedule, and a cover sheet with a locality plan and drawing index.

    DXF

    CAD file

    ASCII DXF (R12) in millimetres, with plan items on separate named layers. Opens in AutoCAD, BricsCAD, DraftSight and LibreCAD.

    3D

    3D model

    GLB as the main format, plus OBJ with MTL in a zip. Opens in Windows 3D Viewer, Blender and most 3D tools.

    PDF

    Siting report

    A short PDF with the site summary, the siting plan with setbacks and dimensions, the chosen design and the site cost estimate. Available now from the export menu.

    Common questions

    Is this approved?

    No. Glide Inside produces concept designs only. They have not been checked against planning controls, the National Construction Code, BASIX or any standard.

    Can I lodge the plans with council?

    No. A qualified designer or certifier must prepare development application or complying development documentation. You can use your concept plan as a starting brief for them.

    How much does Glide Inside Residential cost?

    Drawing and viewing are free. A$30 per job (incl. GST) unlocks a PDF plan set, a DXF for CAD, a 3D model file and an interactive HTML file that opens offline, plus 500 AI credits. A 3D fly-through add-on is A$10 and complex renderings are A$10, made by the Glide Inside team. See a sample plan set

    Do I need an account?

    No account is needed to draw. Projects autosave in your browser and you can save the project file to your computer. To keep designs on any device, saving to a profile is included: no payment or card is needed, and a profile keeps up to 100 designs. When you pay, Stripe collects your email at checkout and we use it to restore your purchase and send your receipt.

    Can I design a granny flat, duplex or shed?

    Yes. There are starting plans for granny flats, duplexes, 4 bedroom houses, sheds and extensions in the plan library, and you can also start from a blank design.

    Can I plan a home extension?

    Yes. Extension mode marks the existing house as existing and your new work as new, and you can place a plan behind your drawing as an underlay to trace it. Glide Inside does not check whether an extension needs approval. Ask your council.

    Can I print the PDF plans more than once?

    Yes. You can export your PDF plans again as often as you like for 12 months, and each export is built from your design as it is when you export. Keep the zip file safe: it holds your plans and files, and you can load it back into the site to carry on with the job.

    What is a credit?

    A credit measures how much of the AI assistant a request uses. A Plan Pack includes 500 AI credits for one project. Credits have no cash value and expire with the Plan Pack. When they run out you can keep editing and exporting by hand.

    What is the refund policy?

    If you change your mind, ask for a refund within 14 days of paying, provided no PDF plan set has been issued for that job. After that, refunds are handled under the Australian Consumer Law, and your rights under it are not affected. See the order terms.
