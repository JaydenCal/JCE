# Commercial and industrial workshop-unit rules dataset (regional NSW)

Generated 13/11/2026 by desktop research. Research only: no contact, sign-ins or purchases. Nothing in this folder is a substitute for the Australian Standards, which are paywalled and were not purchased.

## How to read the data

Every value is an object with `value`, `status`, `source_url`, `clause` and (often) `note`.

| status | meaning |
|---|---|
| VERIFIED_PRIMARY | Read from the primary text: NCC 2022 pages (ABCB, via the cpd.nccb.gov.au mirror) or the council DCP text held in the JCE Dropbox council library |
| VERIFIED_SECONDARY | Read in a council guideline, agency page or report that quotes a standard. The owner must verify against the standard |
| UNVERIFIED | Not confirmed from an accessible primary or official source. Do not rely on it. Candidates are listed with their conflicts |

Files: `accessible.json`, `vehicles.json`, `rates.json`, `councils/tamworth.json`, `councils/gunnedah.json`, `mezzanine.json`, `bollards.json`.

## Key findings that change the brief

1. NCC 2022 is still the edition in force in NSW. Trade press (25/03/2026) reports NSW defers NCC 2025 to 1 May 2027. Confirm on the NSW Planning Portal.
2. The accessible parking clause in NCC 2022 Volume One is D4D6 (it was Table D3.5 in NCC 2019). Class 7 (including 7b), Class 8 and Class 5 are 1 accessible space per 100 carparking spaces or part thereof. Class 6 is 1 per 50 up to 1000 spaces.
3. Gunnedah DCP 2012 was repealed on 18 June 2025 (file name in the JCE library). The current instrument is Gunnedah Shire Council DCP 2025. Both are recorded; use 2025.
4. AS 2890.6:2022 (published 11/11/2022) supersedes AS/NZS 2890.6:2009. The 2400 x 5400 mm bay and 2400 mm shared area are the 2009 values. One WA council table shows 2700 x 5500 mm, which may be the 2022 edition. Which edition NCC 2022 calls up could not be confirmed. Owner must check NCC Schedule 4 (A4) and the standard.
5. AS/NZS 2890.1 and AS 2890.2 dimension tables are paywalled. SRV, MRV, HRV and AV turning radii could not be confirmed and are left null with candidates.
6. No standard or guideline was found for bollards at roller doors or building corners, and none for booster impact protection in NSW.
7. The mezzanine storey rule is NCC 2022 C2D3(4): a mezzanine counts as a storey if its area exceeds 200 m2 or one third of the room floor area, whichever is the lesser.
