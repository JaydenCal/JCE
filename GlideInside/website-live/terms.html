2. The software and price

Glide Inside Residential is a browser tool for drawing a floor plan and viewing it in 3D. Drawing is free and needs no account. A job is unlocked for A$30 including GST, per job, paid once by card through Stripe. Commercial mode is priced separately, see section 10.

3. What a job includes

  • PDF plan sets of your design. You can export again as often as you like for 12 months from purchase; each export is built from the design as it is when you export.
  • DXF, 3D model (GLB and OBJ) and interactive HTML exports for as long as your access to the job lasts. The interactive HTML is one file with the plan and a 3D model that opens offline.
  • 500 AI design assistant credits for that job.

4. Concept design only

Everything produced by Glide Inside is a concept design. It is not for construction, is not a development application or complying development drawing, and is not checked against planning controls, the National Construction Code or BASIX. Dimensions, areas and levels must be verified on site by a suitably qualified person before you rely on them.

Every plan set PDF and DXF file is also subject to the plan set terms of use, which you accept before a file is generated.

5. Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your card details. Prices are in Australian dollars and include GST.

To request a refund, email hello@glideinside.com.au with the email address used for the purchase.

Add-ons such as a 3D fly-through or complex renderings are made by Glide Inside for the price shown when you order. Subdivision modelling is by quote. Pricing and scope are confirmed before work starts.

10. Commercial Developer subscription

  • Commercial mode for developers is priced differently from the A$30 job price above. The Commercial Developer subscription is A$50 per month, including GST, paid by card through Stripe, and gives unlimited commercial plan set PDFs while the subscription is active.
  • The AI design assistant in commercial mode comes with a monthly allowance of messages that renews with each billing period. More messages can be bought as packs, currently A$15 including GST for 100 messages. Unused extra messages carry over while the subscription stays active.
  • The subscription renews monthly until you cancel. You can cancel anytime from Manage billing in your account. Access continues until the end of the period already paid for, and a part month is not refunded unless the Australian Consumer Law requires it.
  • If a payment fails, commercial access continues for 3 days from the first failed payment so you can update your card under Manage billing. If the payment is not fixed by then, commercial access pauses until it is fixed or the subscription ends.
  • Commercial plan sets are concept layouts only, as set out in section 4.
