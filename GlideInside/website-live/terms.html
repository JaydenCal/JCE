2. Pocket Architect and price

Pocket Architect is a browser tool for drawing a floor plan and viewing it in 3D. Drawing is free and needs no account. A job is unlocked for A$35 including GST, per job, paid once by card through Stripe.

3. What a job includes

  • PDF plan sets of your design. You can export again as often as you like for 12 months from purchase; each export is built from the design as it is when you export.
  • DXF, 3D model (GLB and OBJ) and interactive HTML exports for as long as your access to the job lasts. The interactive HTML is one file with the plan and a 3D model that opens offline.
  • 500 AI assistant credits for that job.

4. Concept design only

Everything produced by Pocket Architect is a concept design. It is not for construction, is not a development application or complying development drawing, and is not checked against planning controls, the National Construction Code or BASIX. Dimensions, areas and levels must be verified on site by a suitably qualified person before you rely on them.
