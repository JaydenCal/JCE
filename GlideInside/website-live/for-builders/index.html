How it works

Three steps from your range to a client’s block

You set up once. Every client after that works from your designs and your branding.

  1. Load your designs

    Add your house range and your colours to your builder account. Clients only ever see your own library.

  2. Issue a client login

    Enter the client’s name and, if you know it, their lot. The portal makes the login. You pass it on with your private link or access code.

  3. The client places your home

    They find their block, place your design, adjust it, walk through it and produce your branded PDF.

Builder questions

What is a client login?

A login you issue from your builder portal with the Generate account button. Your client uses it to open Glide Inside with your house library and your branding, find their block and place your designs on it. Client exports are a builder-branded PDF by default.

How do clients reach my portal?

By the private link or access code you send them. There is no public list of builders, so nobody can browse to your portal without it.

Where does the lot data come from?

The lot boundary and neighbouring lots come from the state cadastre (NSW Spatial Services, and the State of Queensland), with aerial imagery and LiDAR contours where the state provides them. Council setbacks are drawn from the council DCP where one is available, otherwise the NSW Codes SEPP Housing Code. In Queensland they follow the Queensland Development Code.

How accurate is it?

The tool is a concept planning aid. Setbacks and other planning controls are indicative only, and the plans are concept only, not for construction. They are not construction drawings or development application documents, and they are not checked against planning controls, the National Construction Code or BASIX. Confirm every control with the council and a suitably qualified person.

Who owns my house designs?

You do. You keep ownership of the designs, logos and other content you add. You give Glide Inside a licence to store, host, display and process them to run your account, for example to show them to your clients and build the files you ask for.

Can other builders or clients see my designs?

No. Clients see only the designs in your own library. The Glide Inside sample designs stay hidden unless you choose to show them, and your designs are never shown to other builders. Authorised Glide Inside staff can see them to support your account.

Can a client download my design files?

By default clients get your branded PDF plans only. You can choose to let them export DXF and 3D model files as well, and you can switch off the option to send plans to JCE.
