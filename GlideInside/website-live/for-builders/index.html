Builder questions

What is a client login?

A login you issue from your builder account with the Generate account button. Your client uses it to open the designer with your house library and place your designs on their own block. Client exports are a builder-branded PDF by default.

Where do the setbacks come from?

Setbacks are drawn from your council’s DCP where one is available, otherwise the NSW Codes SEPP Housing Code. In Queensland they follow the Queensland Development Code. They are indicative only. Confirm them with your council and a suitably qualified person.

Are the plans approved for construction or lodgement?

No. Outputs are concept plans only. They are not construction drawings or development application documents, and they are not checked against planning controls or the National Construction Code.

What does it cost?

Free 14 day trial for builder staff. Then client logins are A$30 incl. During the trial your staff can use everything except issuing client logins and buying AI credit top-ups. When the trial ends the account becomes read only until you add card details. Client logins unlock as soon as you add them. Read the trial terms for the detail.

Which areas does it cover?

New South Wales and Queensland, with a focus on regional areas.
