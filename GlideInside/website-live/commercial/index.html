    Commercial questions

    What does Glide Inside Commercial produce?

    Concept layouts of units, parking and hardstand on the real lot, with the unit count, floor area and site coverage as you go. A Commercial subscription includes commercial plan sets (sheets C000 to C104) and DXF drawings.

    Is the layout checked against planning controls?

    No. Layouts are not checked against planning controls, the National Construction Code or any Australian Standard. Parking, accessible bay and bollard locations are indicative and must be confirmed with council and an access consultant.

    Where do the parking rates come from?

    Commercial parking and accessible bay rates come from NCC 2022 Volume One (accessible parking, D4D6) and council planning documents for Tamworth and Gunnedah. Other areas use a general default. They are indicative, and bay sizes and numbers are not checked against any Australian Standard. As at 13/11/2026.

    What does Commercial Solo include?

    Solo is A$50 per month including GST, for 1 user. It includes unlimited commercial plan set PDFs (sheets C000 to C104), DXF drawings, 150 AI assistant messages each month (extra 100 for A$15) and the auto layouts, car park tool, grading and cut and fill.

    How do I cancel?

    Cancel anytime from Manage billing in your account. Access continues until the end of the period already paid for. The subscription renews monthly until you cancel.
