Pocket Architect, regional NSW and Queensland

Commercial and industrial layoutsBeta

Enter the address of an industrial or commercial lot and lay out rows of units on it. Set the unit size, roller doors, mezzanines and parking, and see the unit count, floor area and site coverage as you go.

Concept layouts only. Setbacks, parking rates and other controls are indicative and must be checked with council.

  • Back to back rows of units that share a party wall, with the unit count and size mix updating as you change the layout.
  • Roller doors, personnel doors, windows, offices and mezzanines for each unit, with clear height checks.
  • Indicative parking and accessible bays, and bollard locations at doors, corners and bays.

Site planning and house design in 3D for regional New South Wales and Queensland.
