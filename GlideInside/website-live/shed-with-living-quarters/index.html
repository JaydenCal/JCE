Planning a shed with living quarters

Pocket Architect does not have a ready made shed home layout. It does have shed templates you can use as the shell, then draw the living quarters inside.

Add rooms and walls, doors and windows, stairs and decks, and place furniture. The 3D model builds as you draw, so you can orbit the shed, take the roof off and walk through it.

See also shed house plans.

Livable shed floor plans for Australia

If you are searching for livable shed floor plans in Australia, treat the template as a quick way to test shed homes plans. Try a bedroom, bathroom and kitchen beside the workshop, and check the layout looks right in 3D.

Whether a shed can be lived in depends on council planning rules and the building code. Pocket Architect does not check this, so ask your council before you plan around it.

Common questions

Can I plan a shed with living quarters?

Yes, as a concept. Open a shed template as the shell and draw the rooms you want inside it. The 3D model updates as you draw.

Can a shed be lived in?

It depends on your council planning rules and the building code. Pocket Architect does not check this. Ask your council before you plan around it.

Which shed templates are available?

There are two. A 9 x 7 m steel garage and workshop shed with two roller doors, and a 15 x 9 m farm machinery shed with three roller doors, 4.2 m walls and a 22,500 L tank.

What do I get for A$35?

One PDF plan set (A3 sheets to scale, with schedules and a title block), a DXF for CAD and a 3D model file (GLB and OBJ) for one job, including GST, plus 500 AI assistant credits. Drawing and viewing are free, and you only pay at the end, when you export.

Do I need to sign up?

No. You can draw and view in 3D without an account. Your work saves in your browser. If you buy a job, Stripe collects your email at checkout and we use it to restore your purchase.
