Worked examples

Three sample lots done in Glide Inside Residential, each with a plan set you can download. Every example is a sample lot with a generic design, exported from the tool, with a sample watermark. Concept only.

Single storey 4 bedroom house

Sample lot, generic design. Concept only.

The default sample house, a single storey 4 bedroom design with a double garage and an alfresco, placed inside the setback envelope of the 1,080 m² sample lot (24 m by 45 m). It has 201 m² of internal floor area, plus the garage and alfresco.

2 bedroom granny flat

Sample lot, generic design. Concept only.

The 2 bedroom granny flat starting plan, 61 m² as drawn, placed on the same sample lot. This sample does not include a driveway. In the tool you can draw one, move the building and resize rooms.

Garage and workshop shed

Sample lot, generic design. Concept only.

The 9 m by 7 m steel garage and workshop shed (63 m²) on the same sample lot, with its driveway. It is the shell to start from when you plan a shed with living quarters: draw the rooms you want inside it.
