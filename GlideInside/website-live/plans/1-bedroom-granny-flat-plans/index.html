    Common questions

    What is a granny flat?

    A granny flat is a small self-contained home on the same property as a main house. The name and the rules differ between states and councils, and in NSW it is often called a secondary dwelling.

    How big is a granny flat?

    The starting plan is 60 square metres and you can resize it. The size allowed on your property depends on your state, your council and your site, so check with your council or the NSW Planning Portal before you settle on a size.

    How much does it cost to build a granny flat?

    Build cost depends on size, finishes, site conditions and the builder, so we do not give a figure. Glide Inside shows the floor area and a room schedule you can take to builders when you ask for quotes.

    Does a granny flat add value?

    That depends on your location, the market and the quality of the build, so we cannot say. A local agent or valuer can advise. A concept plan helps you compare layouts before you decide.

    Can I put a granny flat on my property?

    That depends on your site, your state and your council. Glide Inside lets you test a layout, but it does not check planning rules. Check with your council or the NSW Planning Portal.
