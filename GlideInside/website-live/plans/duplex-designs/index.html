About this starting plan

This starting plan is a duplex made of two mirror image attached units that share a party wall. Each unit has a single garage, open living that opens to a courtyard patio, three bedrooms, a bathroom, an ensuite and a laundry. The 3D model is built when you open it, so you can orbit the pair, take the roofs off and walk through either unit.

Change the room sizes, move the walls, adjust the doors and windows and add furniture and finishes. Duplex designs are easier to discuss once everyone can see the same plan and the same 3D view, whether you are an owner, a small builder or a land developer comparing options.

This is a concept design only.

Common questions

What does the duplex template include?

Two attached 3 bedroom units that mirror each other around a shared party wall, each with a single garage, a courtyard patio, an ensuite and a laundry. You can change any of it.

Can I lodge this with council?

No. It is a concept design, not a development application document. A qualified designer or certifier must prepare the documents your council requires. You can use your concept plan as a starting brief for them.

Do planning rules allow a duplex on my block?

That depends on your council, your zoning and your lot. Pocket Architect does not check planning rules. Ask your council or use the NSW Planning Portal for sites in NSW.

What do I get for A$35?

One PDF plan set, a DXF for CAD and a 3D model file (GLB and OBJ) for one job, including GST, plus 500 AI assistant credits. Drawing and viewing are free.

Do I need to sign up to try it?

No. You can draw and view in 3D without an account. Your work saves in your browser. If you buy a job, Stripe collects your email at checkout and we use it to restore your purchase.
