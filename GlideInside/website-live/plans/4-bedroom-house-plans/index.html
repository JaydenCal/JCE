About this starting plan

This starting plan is a single storey 4 bedroom house of about 200 square metres plus a double garage. It has a lounge, an open plan kitchen, dining and family area that opens to a roofed alfresco, a master bedroom with a walk-in robe and ensuite, three more bedrooms, a bathroom, a separate WC and a laundry.

Open it and the 3D model is already built. Move walls, change room sizes, swap doors and windows, add furniture and pick finishes, then walk through the house in first person. If you prefer two levels, the double storey 4 bedroom template is also available inside the tool.

A plan you can see and walk through makes it easier to compare ideas and brief a designer or builder. This is a concept design only.

Common questions

Is the 4 bedroom plan single or double storey?

The starting plan here is single storey. The tool also has a double storey 4 bedroom template, which you can choose from the template list when you start.

Can I change the layout?

Yes. Walls, rooms, doors, windows, stairs, furniture and finishes can all be edited, and the 3D view updates as you draw.

Does it check the plan against the National Construction Code?

No. Pocket Architect produces concept designs and does not check them against planning controls, the National Construction Code or BASIX. A qualified designer or certifier does that work.

What do I get for A$30?

A PDF plan set, a DXF for CAD, a 3D model file (GLB and OBJ) and an interactive HTML file that opens offline, for one job, including GST, plus 500 AI assistant credits. You can export again as often as you like for 12 months. Drawing and viewing are free.

Do I need to sign up to try it?

No. You can draw and view in 3D without an account. Your work saves in your browser. If you buy a job, Stripe collects your email at checkout and we use it to restore your purchase.
