About this starting plan

This starting plan is an existing 3 bedroom house with a new rear family room and deck. The existing house is marked as existing and the new work is marked as new, so you can see what stays and what changes. One section of the existing rear wall is marked for demolition where the house opens into the new room.

Open it in Glide Inside Residential and change the size of the new room, move the deck, add doors and windows or try a different shape. You can also place your own plan behind the drawing as an underlay and trace it. The 3D model builds as you draw, so you can orbit it and walk through the result.

This helps you compare extension ideas before you talk to a designer or your council. This is a concept design only.

Common questions

How does extension mode work?

The existing house is marked as existing and the new work as new. You can mark a wall for demolition and draw the extension against it, then check the result in 3D.

Can I trace my own house plan?

Yes. You can place a plan behind your drawing as an underlay and trace over it, then add your extension.

Do I need council approval for an extension?

It depends on your site, your state and the size and type of work. Glide Inside does not assess this. Ask your council or use the NSW Planning Portal for sites in NSW.

How much does a home extension cost?

Cost depends on size, finishes, site conditions and the builder, so we do not give a figure. Glide Inside shows floor areas you can take to builders when you ask for quotes.

What do I get for A$30?

A PDF plan set, a DXF for CAD, a 3D model file (GLB and OBJ) and an interactive HTML file that opens offline, for one job, including GST, plus 500 AI assistant credits. You can export again as often as you like for 12 months. Drawing and viewing are free.
