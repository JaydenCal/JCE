Free trial terms

Free 14 day builder trial

Last updated 11/11/2026

These terms are in plain English. They apply to the free trial of the Glide Inside builder portal. When you ask for a trial you tick a box to say you agree to them.

1. Who runs the trial

Glide Inside is operated by JCE Infrastructure Pty Ltd trading as Glide Inside, ABN 29 699 165 663. Contact: hello@glideinside.com.au or 0477 207 027.

2. Who can apply

  • Builders and building businesses with an Australian Business Number (ABN), working in New South Wales or Queensland.
  • One trial for each business. We check the ABN, and we may decline or end a trial if the details are wrong or the portal is being misused.

3. Approval

You ask for a trial on the For builders page. We look at every request ourselves and approve each one before any account exists. We aim to reply within one business day. If we approve it, we create your account and a first administrator login for the contact on the request, and we pass the username and temporary password to you ourselves. The system does not send automatic emails to you. We may decline a request without giving a reason.

4. How long it lasts

The trial is 14 days. The day we approve your request counts as day 1, and the trial runs to the end of the 14th day, Sydney time. For example, a trial approved on 11/11/2026 runs to the end of 24/11/2026. We may extend a trial, for example by 7 days, if we choose to.

5. What your staff can use during the trial

  • Your house library and the Glide Inside library.
  • Your branding on plans.
  • Pocket Architect, including Find my block and indicative setbacks.
  • The AI design helper, within the monthly allowance (300 credits for each login each month).
  • PDF and DXF exports.
  • Send to JCE, if you choose to send a design to JCE Infrastructure Pty Ltd for engineering or development application plans.

The trial is for your own staff. It costs nothing and no card is needed to ask for it.

6. What is not available during the trial

  • Client logins. You cannot generate, reset or enable a client login until you add billing details.
  • AI credit top-ups. The monthly allowance still applies.

7. When the trial ends

When the 14 days are over the account becomes read only. You can still sign in, view your projects and open them. You cannot make changes, save, export, use the AI helper or issue client logins until you add card details. You add a card through a secure Stripe page. We do not see or store your card number. As soon as the card is saved the account becomes a normal paid account and everything works again.

8. Pricing after the trial

  • A$30 including GST for each new client login (A$25 including GST each after the first 15 in a calendar month, and a rate agreed with you above 50 in a month), charged once, when your client first signs in. Nothing is charged for a login that is never used.
  • A$10 including GST for each top-up of 300 AI credits.
  • No monthly fee.
  • Charges are added up each calendar month and taken from the card on file at the start of the next month. You receive a tax invoice from JCE Infrastructure Pty Ltd trading as Glide Inside, ABN 29 699 165 663, made out to your legal business name and ABN.

Prices are in Australian dollars. We will tell you before any price changes for your account.

9. Your data

If your trial ends and you do not add billing details, we keep your account and designs for 90 days after the trial ends, so you can come back to them. After that we may delete them. You can ask us to delete your data sooner at any time by emailing us.

10. Concept designs only, and indicative setbacks

Everything produced in Pocket Architect is a concept design. It is not for construction, is not a development application or complying development drawing, and is not checked against planning controls, the National Construction Code or BASIX. Setbacks and other planning controls shown are indicative only. They are drawn from council documents or state code rules that may be out of date or may not apply to a particular lot. Check them with the relevant council and a suitably qualified person before you rely on them.

11. Your designs and our licence

You keep ownership of the house designs, logos and other content you add to your account. You give Glide Inside a licence to store, host, display and process that content for the purpose of running your account, for example to show your designs to your clients and to build the files you ask for. We do not use your designs for anything else and we do not show them to other builders. The Glide Inside library, the software and the data sources remain ours or our suppliers’.

12. Privacy

We use the details you give in your request to assess it, to set up your trial and to contact you about it. We do not sell them. See the privacy policy.

13. Ending the trial early

You can stop at any time by emailing us, and we will delete your account and data if you ask. We may end a trial early if these terms are broken.

14. Liability, changes and law

Our goods and services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. Nothing in these terms excludes them. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for loss arising from reliance on concept designs as construction or approval documents. We may update these terms; the version on this page when your trial is approved applies to that trial. These terms are governed by the law of New South Wales.
