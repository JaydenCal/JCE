## 7. Automated cut and fill calculations (Acknowledgement: `cut_fill_indicative_only`)

7.1 The Service builds terrain models (including triangulated irregular networks, TINs) from third-party LiDAR, contour and other elevation sources and computes earthworks volumes, including by prismoidal methods, relative to a proposed or assumed design level or slab level.

7.2 You acknowledge that cut and fill results:

(a) depend on the accuracy, date, resolution and coverage of source data, which we do not control. LiDAR-derived models carry vertical error (commonly stated in source metadata) that varies with vegetation, ground cover, slope, surface type and processing, and contour-derived models carry interpolation error between contour lines;
(b) may not reflect post-capture changes to the site, including excavations, fills, structures, stockpiles, drainage works and vegetation;
(c) are affected by TIN construction, interpolation, grid resolution and boundary effects, and different methods yield different volumes;
(d) are bank (in-situ) volumes unless stated otherwise, and do not account for bulking, shrinkage, compaction, over-excavation, batters, working space, trenches, footings, pad build-up, topsoil stripping, rock, contaminated or unsuitable material, spoil handling or disposal;
(e) make assumptions about design levels, batter slopes and extents that you must check; and
(f) are not suitable, without independent verification by a qualified surveyor or engineer, for **pricing, tendering, quantity surveying, ordering of materials, haulage or disposal, programme or contractual measurement, or for any earthworks, excavation or construction**.

7.3 You must independently verify levels and volumes (for example by a detailed or feature survey) before relying on any cut/fill Output for any purpose beyond early feasibility.
