### Known weaknesses (SoEE)
- Primary output uses the OFFLINE FIXTURE layers (approximate geometry, saved run). It must be re-run against live SA9 data before any real use. The all-NOT-CHECKED variant shows the unqueried case.
- Not obtained (so NOT REVIEWED): EP&A Act, EP&A Regulation, Gunnedah LEP 2012, Housing and Codes SEPPs, Planning for Bush Fire Protection 2019, the scanned Floodplain Management Plan (no text layer). The section 4.15(1) headings follow the Act as recalled and are marked for checking against the current Act. SEPPs named by the mapping data are named only.
- Requirement values were extracted by a model and are not human verified (some confidence below 1). The width PASS rests on a PLATFORM DEFAULT width, and the stabilised access PASS rests on an indicative ESC layout value.
- Compliance covers only controls present in the knowledge files (21 rows). No cited value exists for driveway grade, batters, legal point of discharge or lot specific flood level, so those stay UNRESOLVED.
- The ESC layout is indicative, uses DEM levels with no building footprint, and does not place stockpiles or laydown (NOT DRAWN, stated in the SoEE). Driveway earthworks only (cut 1.1 m3, fill 0.0 m3 INDICATIVE); the swept path is not drawn on C400.
- Fall figures differ slightly between SA10 (2.7%, bearing 46) and the DEM note (2.6%, bearing 45); both are stated and flagged as a fit to different site DEM levels.
- Extraction depends on plan set helpers and `terrain_notes`. Each call is guarded: if a signature changes, the item falls back to DATA NOT AVAILABLE (tests expecting the PASS rows would then fail, which is the intended alarm).
- Street address, adjoining land use, existing buildings and trees, easements, services, traffic, contamination, social and economic effects and the public interest are DATA NOT AVAILABLE or NOT ASSESSED.
- DOCX pagination was checked through LibreOffice; Word may break pages slightly differently. The DOCX contents list has no page numbers (PDF has). The stranded heading check is a text heuristic.
