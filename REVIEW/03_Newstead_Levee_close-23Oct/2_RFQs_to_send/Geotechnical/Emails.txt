To: info@geotestsouthern.com.au
Subject: RFQ: Newstead Levee Design 26/27 (Mount Alexander Shire Council RFQ M2025-125): Geotechnical investigation and design advice (provisional sum), quote due Fri 16/10/2026 12:00pm

Dear Geotechnical Testing Services team,

JCE Infrastructure is tendering for Mount Alexander Shire Council's Newstead Levee Design 26/27 (RFQ M2025-125), which closes at 2:00 pm on Friday 23/10/2026. The work is the detailed design, approvals support and construction tender package for the raised, extended and realigned Newstead levee (Option 6b), with the final package due on Friday 26/02/2027.

I would like your firm to quote the geotechnical investigation and design advice for the Newstead levee, as a sub-consultant to JCE. A Bendigo-based geotechnical consultant and laboratory would also help our local content response to Council.

JCE will carry this work as a provisional sum that proceeds only if Council directs it after the Design Basis Memorandum (HP2), so itemised prices and unit rates matter. Please price a lean Stage 1 (8 locations on the existing levee, focused on the southern section) separately from Stage 2 (the new and realigned sections).

Please send your quotation by 12:00 pm (AEDT) on Friday 16/10/2026 to jayden@jceinfrastructure.com.au, priced against the items in Section 6.0 of the attached RFQ, with hourly rates, your assumptions and exclusions, and validity of at least 90 days. Council requires public liability of $20 million, professional indemnity of $5 million held for five years after completion, and workers compensation; please attach certificates of currency or tell me if your cover differs. Please also declare any conflict of interest, including whether you are working with another tenderer on this RFQ.

The RFQ and Council's brief are confidential and are provided only for your quotation. Any engagement depends on JCE winning the contract. If you have questions, please call me on 0477 207 027 or reply by 5:00 pm Wednesday 14/10/2026.

Kind regards,
Jayden Callen
Director | JCE Infrastructure Pty Ltd
0477 207 027 | jayden@jceinfrastructure.com.au
