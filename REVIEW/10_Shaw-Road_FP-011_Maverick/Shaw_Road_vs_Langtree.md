Prepared 12/10/2026 for Jayden Callen. Internal working note, not for issue to the client in this form.

## Read this first

- **Langtree FP-BL0979 (23/02/2026) is for 325 Shaw Road, Lot 19 on SP107219 (2.58 ha, service station and car wash), not for 369 Shaw Road, Lot 16 (14.62 ha).** Its figures are a benchmark for scope and price, not a like-for-like quote. Lot 16 has a waterway through it, about 5.7 times the land, a 600 m internal road with a culvert crossing and about 10 ha of fill platforms.
- Langtree's proposal names "Jayden Callen (Senior Civil Designer)" as part of its delivery team. Jayden was employed by Langtree from 03/2025 to 05/2026. **Jayden to check his Langtree employment terms (confidentiality, non-solicitation or restraint) before issuing.** Maverick supplied the quote himself, and nothing in Rev B relies on Langtree material other than that quote.
- Langtree's flood lead is Dr Mark Jempson of Venant Solutions, the same firm JCE proposes. Ask Venant about any conflict or shared work on Lot 19 (RFQ asks this).
- All figures exclude GST. JCE sub-consultant provisional sums include JCE's 10% margin.

## Value points JCE can make (without criticising Langtree)

- One principal-led team from flood study to For Construction set, with two hold points before the footprint is fixed.
- Three defined footprint options against two; the SC6.7 event set (both critical durations, 0.2% AEP, climate change, sensitivity) priced in.
- MUSIC and RFI allowances shown, with no internal contradiction between scope and exclusions.
- Servicing feasibility with Townsville Water before the footprint is fixed, so the DA layout is not redone later.
- Every specialist cost visible as a provisional sum with the quotation to follow.

## Risks to the price position

- Venant's quote (PS1 $18,000) is the swing item. If it comes in above about $18,500, JCE moves above Langtree's $57,500 on the DA package. Options: trim JCE's A4 or B3, or explain the larger event set and third option.
- The TIA PS ($10,000) mirrors Langtree's own allowance. Update when quotes arrive.
- The Sunny Lotay RPEQ allowance (DA $3,000; OW $7,000) is an estimate at $200/hr, to be confirmed with Sunny.
