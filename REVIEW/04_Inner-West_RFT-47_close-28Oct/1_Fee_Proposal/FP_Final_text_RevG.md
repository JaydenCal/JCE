## 1.0   Project understanding

Council seeks lump sum pricing for a comprehensive condition audit and revaluation of its transport asset network, with documented programs for capital renewal, upgrade and maintenance works. The work validates and updates existing data and spatial locations, collects new or discovered assets, records physical defects, supplies updated ESRI shapefiles, and revalues the nominated asset classes under AASB 13, AASB 116, AASB 136, AASB 2022-10 and the IPWEA guidance notes on asset valuation.

### What the work must resolve

- **A register Council can rely on.** Every asset linked to Council's Asset_ID, new assets given unique IDs, and spatial data free of geometry errors, ready for TechnologyOne EAM and IntraMaps or QGIS.
- **Defects and maintenance.** Very High and Extreme risk defects reported to Council the same day, road defects reported weekly, and a footpath maintenance program that separates grinding (lifts under 35 mm), asphalt wedges (over 35 mm), paver resetting, slab replacement and permanent restoration.
- **Renewal planning.** Capital renewal, upgrade and maintenance programs built from interval condition data (50 m, 20 m and 10 m), not overall ratings alone.
- **A defensible valuation.** Replacement costs verified against Rawlinsons and Council's own contract rates, condition-based remaining lives, and complete working papers for Council's auditors.
- **Time.** Award in late November 2026 and completion by 31/03/2027 leaves about 17 working weeks after the Christmas shutdown, so the revaluation methodology, unit rates and pilot run in parallel with the field program (Section 4.0).

### Stage 1: Inception, stakeholder meeting, capture forms and methodology

- Two-hour inception meeting at Council with JCE's agenda and minutes, and the initial stakeholder meeting on data fields, submission requirements and the audit and review process.
- Review of Council's asset registers, attributes and lookup codes, State roads list, defect types, spatial layers, ECW aerial imagery and condition guidelines, with a gap list back to Council within five business days.
- Capture forms and data definitions for each asset class, condition criteria (1 Very Good to 5 Very Poor, with photographic examples), defect types and risk ratings, the segment convention (left side from the "From" street to the "To" street, with house number sub-segments) and the audit and QA plan, issued for Council's written approval.
- Field trial with Council representatives to test the forms and definitions before the pilot.


### Stage 2: Pilot data collection

- Two-week pilot in Council's nominated area, with the sample data made available for Council's review.
- JCE checks the sample against the definitions, revises the forms, and holds the final review meeting with Council at JCE's cost. Network capture starts only after Council's written approval.


### Stage 3: Network inventory and condition audit

- Roads (464 km): inventory, widths with concrete and asphalt sections separated, overall and 50 m interval condition, and defects; regional roads in both directions.
- Footpaths (844 km): footpath and verge widths and areas, overall and 20 m interval condition, defects, and lift heights measured for the footpath maintenance program.
- Kerb and gutter (865.9 km): overall and 10 m interval condition and defects.
- Traffic devices with dimensions, street furniture, artwork, car park surfaces and assets, white way lighting, signage by type, bicycle routes and lanes: inventory checked against the register, new assets added, condition and defects; and any optional classes Council selects.
- Very High and Extreme risk defects emailed to Council's contact the same day with the defect type, location and an image; road defects such as potholes reported weekly, or as agreed at inception.
- JCE field verification and partner audit visit in February 2027, and weekly desktop re-rating of a random 5% of the partner's segments and assets from its imagery, with feedback and retraining of the partner's raters.


### Stage 4: Data QA, deliverables, reports and workshop

- JCE builds in-house, from the partner's weekly data drops: Excel database and ESRI shapefiles with Asset_ID and unique IDs, data dictionary and metadata (Section 2.1), and asset register update files (additions, disposals and attribute changes) for TechnologyOne EAM.
- Initial asset inventory report: findings, condition issues, and risk assessment and risk management recommendations, with condition and defect maps.
- Footpath maintenance program and capital renewal, upgrade and maintenance programs.
- Four-hour workshop for Council staff, led and minuted by JCE, with documents and a detailed agenda issued seven days before; then the final report with executive summary.
- Every document signed by Jayden Callen as Prime Contractor's project manager, certifying it was prepared by competent professional staff and checked for accuracy, compliance with the relevant regulations and the project scope.


### Stage 5: Comprehensive revaluation

- Revaluation methodology report (by 22/01/2027): valuation date as Council confirms, componentisation, valuation units, fair value hierarchy, and treatment under AASB 13, AASB 116, AASB 136 and AASB 2022-10, following the IPWEA guidance.
- Current replacement cost unit rates from Rawlinsons, Council's contract rates and internal data, each verified and referenced in the working papers; useful lives, residual values and condition to remaining life relationships.
- Revaluation calculations for every asset in the nine classes marked for revaluation, reconciled to Council's register; draft and final revaluation reports with working papers by 31/03/2027, reviewed and signed by the partner's qualified valuer.


### Stage 6: Project management and quality

- Single point of contact, monthly progress reports, management of the partners' programs, WHS and quality, Director review of every deliverable, Conserve and EFTsure registration, data handover and close-out.
- Fortnightly PCG meetings on Microsoft Teams to 31/03/2027 with JCE's agenda, minutes and action list (Pricing Schedule Item 2).
