# Questions for the buyer (RFIs): VP523438, MBDC RFT 2026.013 Concept Design for Hawthorn Road

FINAL 12/10/2026. Not lodged. Asking is Jayden's call.

**Cut-off:** Tuesday 27/10/2026, 5:00 pm Adelaide time (5:30 pm NSW time). **How:** in writing via VendorPanel (Brief section 14), Members area > the tender > questions. Lodge by Wednesday 21/10/2026 so the answers arrive before the sub-consultant quotes (due 23/10/2026) are folded in.

**Change from the 11/10/2026 draft:** the Public Liability question (old question 2) is removed, because the final fee proposal commits JCE to lift PL to $20 million on award (Win Themes v2). The remaining seven questions are unchanged in substance.

1. The Brief and the draft Services Agreement do not state the evaluation criteria or their weightings. Could Council please publish the criteria and weightings, including the weighting given to price?
2. Brief section 2 describes Separable Portion 2 as 30% through to IFC with 50%, 70% and 100% hold points, Brief 9(g) asks for PC Sums for 70% Detailed Design and 100% IFC, and Agreement Items 4 and 5 show SP2 (Detailed Design 70%) and SP3 (IFC 100%). Should tenderers provide two PC Sums (SP2 to 70%, SP3 covering 100%, IFA, IFT and IFC), and should procurement and construction phase support be priced or left to the hourly rates?
3. What traffic data will Council provide at appointment (AADT, heavy vehicle percentages, turning movement counts at the key intersections, crash history), and is the consultant expected to commission additional traffic counts for the SIDRA modelling and pavement design?
4. For the engineering survey (Brief 3.3), does Council require true property boundaries to be re-established by a licensed surveyor along the whole corridor, or is cadastral information adjusted to fences acceptable at concept stage, with boundary definition limited to the land acquisition properties? Is underground services location to Quality Level B (AS 5488) expected?
5. Brief 6 lists a Road Safety Audit in the concept deliverables. At which stages should audits be allowed for (preliminary 30% and final concept?), and does Council require the audit team leader to be on DIT's list of Senior Road Safety Auditors and independent of the consultant's organisation?
6. Can Council confirm the current design status of DIT's Adelaide Road / Hawthorn Road intersection upgrade and the link road and roundabout options, and that their designs (CAD) and the approved #73 Hawthorn Road CHR design will be available at start-up so the concept can tie in?
7. Clause 12 of the draft Agreement contains an indemnity and release with no limit of liability. Will Council consider a cap on the consultant's liability (for example equal to the $2 million Professional Indemnity cover required by Item 14) and an indemnity reduced to the extent of the consultant's negligence, or would that be treated as a non-conforming departure? Also, clause 20.15.1 requires Return to Work SA registration while clause 10.4 applies it "where required by law": please confirm that an interstate consultant with no workers in South Australia may instead provide evidence of cover under clause 20.15.6.
