## 3. Local preference
- Council applies a 5% Local Preference discount to relevant suppliers under its Sustainable Procurement and Contracts Policy. The response form defines local presence as a **shop front and/or depot in the Mid-Western Regional Council LGA**.
- JCE has no shop front or depot in the LGA. The form is answered **No**, with no address. Jayden has confirmed not to pursue local preference.
- The policy text could not be opened from here, so whether a local subconsultant's office counts is unconfirmed. Navigate Planning's office location was not confirmed by web search. Do not claim local presence for JCE.
- Price strategy: a local competitor's price is discounted by 5% in evaluation, so JCE is about $7,440 behind a local firm at the same price. Price is likely to be heavily weighted on an RFQ of this kind. Options if the planner's quote comes in high: drop or reduce the QS allowance if Council holds facility costs; reduce the in-person trip cost; trim the risk allowance.
